## Privacy Policy

The privacy of my visitors is extremely important. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected and how it is used.

First and foremost, I will never share your email address or any other personal information to anyone without your direct consent.

### Log Files

Like many other websites, this site keeps a record of page views to help learn when, from where, and how often traffic flows to this site. Each record includes:

* The page you visited, and the date and time
* Approximate location (city, region, country) derived from your network
* Your Internet Service Provider or network operator
* Browser, operating system, device type, and preferred language
* The site that referred you, if any

None of this is linked to anything personally identifiable, and none of it is shared with or sold to anyone.

### Cookies and Web Beacons

This site sets no cookies of its own. There are no comments, no advertising, and no third-party ad servers.

A small number of static assets (web fonts, math typesetting) are loaded from third-party content delivery networks, which necessarily see the IP address of your browser.

#### Analytics

This site is served through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), and I record a small amount of information about each page view to understand which pages people read. This happens on the server, so there is no tracking script in your browser and nothing is stored on your device.

Your IP address is **not** recorded. It is converted into a short hash using a secret key that changes every day, which lets me count distinct visitors within a single day without being able to recognise you the next day or link you across days.

If you wish to disable cookies, you may do so through your web browser options. Instructions for doing so can be found on the specific web browsers' websites.
